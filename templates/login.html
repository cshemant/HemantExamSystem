Frequently asked questions

Good to know.

Quick answers for students, faculty members and institutions considering the platform.

What is Learn with Hemant Exam System?

It is an exam management platform for creating, scheduling, conducting and evaluating assessments, with dedicated workflows for faculty, administrators and students.

Can examinations work without the internet?

Yes. An offline Windows package supports LAN-ready, internet-independent examination setups, in addition to the online platform.

Does it support randomized questions and automatic scoring?

Yes. The platform supports randomized papers and automated scoring for supported question types, with faculty review for responses that require manual evaluation.

Is the platform only for theory exams?

No. It also provides workflows for practical assessments, viva, attendance and practical marks.

Does AI monitoring guarantee a cheat-proof exam?

No. AI-assisted monitoring provides alerts and review signals. Human supervision and suitable exam policies remain important.
