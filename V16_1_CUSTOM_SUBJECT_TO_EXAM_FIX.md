# V16.1 — Custom Subject to Exam Flow

This update connects custom subjects in the Subject Catalog to the examination approval flow.

## Correct workflow
1. Add a custom subject in **Question Bank → Subject Catalog**.
2. Open the subject workspace.
3. Add questions manually or upload CSV/Excel.
4. Approve the required questions.
5. In the selected subject workspace, choose duration and questions per student and click **Create Draft Exam**.
6. The subject now appears on **Exams** as a Draft / Inactive exam.
7. Use **Request Approval / Approve** and then **Activate**.

A Subject Catalog entry being active is different from an Exam being approved and active. Adding a subject alone no longer leaves the user without a clear path to create an exam.
