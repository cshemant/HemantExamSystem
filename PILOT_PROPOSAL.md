# Pilot Proposal — Hybrid Examination Management System

## Objective
Validate a complete offline/LAN examination workflow in one department before campus-wide adoption.

## Proposed pilot scope
- 1 department
- 1–2 subjects
- 50–100 students
- 1 computer lab / existing college Wi-Fi or LAN
- Faculty onboarding and one live examination

## Pilot workflow
1. Configure institution branding.
2. Create batch / section.
3. Bulk import student logins.
4. Activate or import Question Bank.
5. Create exam blueprint and randomized pool.
6. Assign batch, schedule and venue.
7. Faculty requests approval; HOD / Exam Controller approves.
8. Open Exam Centre page and share LAN QR code.
9. Students attempt exam without Internet.
10. Review results, integrity signals and class analytics.
11. Export results and take encrypted backup.

## Success criteria
- Students can access the exam reliably on the LAN.
- No Internet dependency during the examination.
- Answers autosave and attempts can resume.
- Results are generated correctly.
- Faculty can export results and review activity monitoring.
- HOD can view pass percentage and academic analytics.

## Deployment note
For production Windows distribution, use the installer build and a trusted code-signing certificate. Do not instruct users to bypass Windows security warnings.
